# Claims ledger

How to read this file.

- Every row is one empirical claim about an option strategy, with where it came from.
- The study environment could not open papers or publisher pages (the egress policy blocks almost all hosts). All literature numbers were obtained from web-search result summaries that cite the listed sources. They are second-hand: "as reported by" the source, not re-derived.
- Status codes: `S` = reported in a search-result summary of the cited source; `S2` = reported consistently by two or more independent sources; `R` = recalled from background knowledge, not confirmed in this session; `D` = reproduced by code in this repo on real data (see `results/`).
- "Net of costs" records whether the source says the result survives transaction costs. Most results are gross.
- Where a summary was ambiguous, the ambiguity is kept in the row.
