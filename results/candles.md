# Candlestick price action results

Generated by `scripts/run_candles.py`. Pattern rules are in `ema_lab/patterns.py`. A pattern is detected at a bar's close and traded from the next bar's open.

**Success percentage (hit rate)** = share of pattern occurrences where price moved in the predicted direction between the next open and the close h bars later. Each is compared with the base rate for any bar (`all` rows) or for any bar with the same prior trend (`in context` rows: reversal patterns after a move against them, continuation patterns after a move with them). `t` is the t-statistic of the excess mean return over that base rate with standard errors clustered by week (daily) or session (15m). |t| > 2 is the usual bar, but with 22 patterns x 2 scopes x 3 horizons x 4 universes, expect a handful of |t| > 2 results from luck alone.
